Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy.

How G-NeMa collects, uses, protects and discloses information across its website, services and G-NeMa SEO Intelligence application.

Effective date: September 9, 2026

1. Who we are

G-NeMa (Galactic Nexus of Machines) operates https://gnema.in/ and develops intelligent software products and systems. In this policy, “G-NeMa,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to G-NeMa and the services operated by G-NeMa.

2. Information we collect

Website and enquiry information. If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, role, message and other enquiry details.

Technical information. Our hosting, security and analytics infrastructure may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring URL, requested pages and timestamps. We use this information for security, reliability, troubleshooting and service improvement.

G-NeMa SEO Intelligence. If you authorize G-NeMa SEO Intelligence to access a Google Search Console property, the application receives only the Google Search Console data permitted by the OAuth scope you approve. The current integration requests the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly.

3. How we use Google Search Console data

G-NeMa SEO Intelligence uses authorized Google Search Console data solely to provide SEO performance and website intelligence for the authorized property. This can include search queries, pages, clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position and related performance dimensions returned by the Search Console API.

We use this information to generate performance summaries, identify SEO opportunities, diagnose technical visibility issues and improve the G-NeMa website and its content strategy.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google Search Console data for advertising, creditworthiness, employment decisions or unrelated profiling. We do not use the data for purposes that are incompatible with the authorization you provide.

4. Google OAuth authorization

When you connect G-NeMa SEO Intelligence to Google, Google handles the authentication and consent process. G-NeMa receives authorization credentials necessary to make the approved API requests. Access can be revoked from your Google Account or through Google Search Console permissions.

We request the narrowest Search Console permission needed for the reporting function: read-only access. We do not request permission to modify or delete Search Console data.

5. Storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. OAuth credentials and other secrets used for automated operations are intended to be stored in protected secret-management systems rather than public source code.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to reduce security risks and limit access to information to what is necessary for the service.

6. Data sharing and service providers

We may use trusted infrastructure and service providers for hosting, source control, automation, security, email or other operational functions. Such providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or to protect the rights, safety and security of G-NeMa, our users or others.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining security, meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes. Retention periods can vary by the type of information and the operational or legal requirement.

Search Console performance data used by G-NeMa SEO Intelligence may be retained in reports or operational records for the purpose of website and SEO analysis. We aim to keep such records proportionate to the reporting need.

8. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request information about personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask about deletion where applicable. You can also revoke Google authorization through your Google Account. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and accounting purposes.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

G-NeMa may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, security or analytics. Browser settings can generally be used to control cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business and general audiences and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the service.

11. International use

G-NeMa operates in a global digital environment. Information may be processed in countries where G-NeMa or its service providers operate, subject to applicable data-protection requirements and appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products, legal obligations or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
