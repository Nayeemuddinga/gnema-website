G-NeMa Research Lens

Intelligence becomes infrastructure.

G-NeMa approaches emerging technologies as systems problems: computation, data, agents, interfaces, physical environments and governance need to work together. The objective is not simply to deploy a model, but to engineer an intelligence layer that can operate safely and measurably in the real world.
