Technology direction · systems architecture · responsible engineering

Engineering questions

From concept to system.

G-NeMa studies this frontier as an engineering discipline: what is technically possible, what infrastructure it requires, where the constraints sit, and how humans remain able to understand and govern the resulting system.

01

Signal acquisition

BCI systems capture electrical, magnetic or other measurable correlates of neural activity.

02

Decoding

Machine-learning models map noisy neural signals to intended commands or states.

03

Assistive interaction

Potential applications include communication, mobility and accessibility technologies.

04

Closed-loop systems

Future systems can combine neural input with adaptive feedback and external actuators.

05

Safety and consent

Neural interfaces require rigorous privacy, medical, security and human-agency controls.

G-NeMa architecture view

Intelligence is a stack, not a single model.

Our research connects computation, data, models, agents, interfaces, physical systems and governance. Each frontier becomes useful when its components can operate together under explicit constraints and observable outcomes.

For enterprise and mission environments, the design target is controlled capability: measurable performance, traceability, resilience and clear human authority.
