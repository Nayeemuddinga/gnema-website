Engineering questions

From concept to system.

G-NeMa studies this frontier as an engineering discipline: what is technically possible, what infrastructure it requires, where the constraints sit, and how humans remain able to understand and govern the resulting system.

01

Event-driven processing

Computation can be triggered by changes in signals rather than continuous dense processing.

02

Energy efficiency

Sparse activity and specialized hardware can reduce the energy required for certain workloads.

03

Edge intelligence

Neuromorphic approaches are relevant to always-on sensing and constrained devices.

04

Robotics

Low-latency perception and control are natural research targets for event-driven architectures.

05

Hardware-software co-design

The architecture spans sensors, processors, learning rules, models and runtime systems.
