Data Deletion.

Instructions for asking G-NeMa to delete information linked to your use of our services.

Updated: November 22, 2026

Request removal of your data

To request deletion of personal data associated with your use of G-NeMa services or a connected Meta/Instagram account, email privacy@gnema.in with the subject G-NeMa Data Deletion Request. State which G-NeMa service you used and the account or email to which the request relates. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or government identity documents in the initial request.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request comes from the relevant account owner. We will review the request, delete eligible personal data under our control, or explain what must be retained to meet legal, security, or contractual obligations. We will respond using the contact details provided.

You can also remove G-NeMa's access to your Instagram or Facebook account through Meta's account integrations and permissions settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but may not itself delete previously supplied information; contact us to request deletion where applicable.

G-NeMa's current Instagram gateway is a callback relay and does not itself store access tokens or authorization codes. If other G-NeMa services have processed your data, please identify those services in your request.
