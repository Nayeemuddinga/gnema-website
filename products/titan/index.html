THE TITAN PRINCIPLE

AI proposes.
Deterministic controls decide.

TITAN is designed around a simple enterprise boundary: intelligence can recommend an action, but policy, database permissions and execution controls determine whether anything can happen.

06 / FAQ

Questions before
you deploy?

Start safely. You do not need to give TITAN autonomous production write access on day one.

Can TITAN start in read-only mode?

Yes. The assessment is designed as a read-only engagement. Pilot deployments can also begin with observation and recommendation before controlled execution is enabled.

Does TITAN replace a DBA?

No. TITAN is designed to augment database and infrastructure teams by reducing investigation time, correlating signals and preparing evidence-backed actions.

Will AI directly execute any SQL?

AI output is not the authorization layer. Candidate actions must pass the configured deterministic policy controls and the deployment's approval and privilege model.

Which Oracle environments can be assessed?

Compatibility depends on the Oracle version, workload and access available for the engagement. We confirm the environment during qualification before deployment.

Is TITAN “SOX/GDPR/HIPAA compliant”?

TITAN provides auditability and security controls that can support an organization's compliance program. Compliance is a shared responsibility and depends on the customer's environment, processes and applicable obligations.

What happens after the pilot?

You receive a results and evidence report. If TITAN demonstrates value, you can move to a managed deployment or an enterprise agreement with a scope appropriate to your database estate.
