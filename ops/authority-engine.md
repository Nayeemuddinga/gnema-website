# G-NeMa Authority Engine — 90-Day Plan

## Objective
Build durable organic-search authority and product discovery around G-NeMa's existing 29-page technical foundation. The engine prioritizes useful technical content, contextual internal links, Search Console measurement, and product-led conversion paths.

## Operating cadence
- Publish 2 Insights articles per week for 10 weeks, then use the final 10 days for consolidation, refreshes, and performance-driven updates.
- Review Search Console weekly: impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, queries, pages and indexing.
- Review internal links after every article publication.
- Do not chase rankings from a single measurement; use 28-day and 90-day trends.

## 90-day phases

### Days 1–14 — Foundation
1. Finalize topic clusters and article templates.
2. Add hub-to-article and article-to-product links.
3. Establish baseline Search Console metrics for the 29 current URLs.
4. Publish articles 1–4.
5. Validate canonical, sitemap and structured data after each batch.

### Days 15–30 — Enterprise AI authority
1. Publish articles 5–8.
2. Strengthen links into Enterprise AI Operating Layer and Multi-Agent Organization.
3. Build Enterprise AI and Multi-Agent AI clusters around the Insights hub.
4. Record query impressions that reveal new search language.

### Days 31–45 — Database intelligence authority
1. Publish articles 9–12.
2. Build Oracle Database AI → TITAN pathways.
3. Add contextual links from Solutions, Research and Industries where relevant.
4. Identify high-impression/low-CTR pages for title/snippet refinement.

### Days 46–60 — Frontier authority
1. Publish articles 13–16.
2. Strengthen Space AI → ORBITALIS/SENTINEL ORBIT and Robotics AI → FORGE/ATLAS clusters.
3. Connect frontier content to Research and Solutions.

### Days 61–75 — Security and defense authority
1. Publish articles 17–20.
2. Strengthen AI Cybersecurity → SENTINEL CYBER and Defense AI → AEGIS DEFENSE paths.
3. Use cautious, technically defensible language for high-consequence domains.

### Days 76–90 — Consolidation and conversion
1. Review all article performance in Search Console.
2. Refresh pages with emerging impressions and weak CTR.
3. Strengthen the best-performing article → product → contact journeys.
4. Add missing internal links discovered from query/page data.
5. Produce a 90-day authority report with wins, opportunities and next-quarter topics.

### Linking rules
- Every article: 2–4 contextual internal links.
- At least one relevant product link where a product exists.
- At least one link back to the Insights hub or related cluster article.
- Use descriptive anchor text; avoid repetitive exact-match anchors.
- Never link to the legacy `products/titan.html`; use `/products/titan/`.

## Search Console tracking
Track weekly and compare 28-day periods. Priority fields:
- Query
- Landing page
- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Average position
- First seen date
- 28-day change
- 90-day change
- Intent: informational / commercial / navigational
- Cluster
- Recommended action

### Opportunity rules
- High impressions + low CTR → improve title/description and alignment with query intent.
- Positions roughly 5–20 → prioritize content expansion and internal links.
- New query with meaningful impressions → evaluate a dedicated article or section.
- Multiple pages receiving the same query → assess cannibalization and consolidate/link intentionally.
- No impressions → do not repeatedly request indexing; improve content/internal discovery and wait for crawl data.

## Content standard
Each article should be genuinely useful rather than a keyword page. Recommended structure:
1. Search-intent answer in the opening section.
2. Clear architecture or process explanation.
3. Practical considerations and limitations.
4. Governance/safety considerations where relevant.
5. G-NeMa perspective grounded in the company's product architecture.
6. Contextual internal links.
7. Clear but non-hyped CTA to the relevant product or Contact page.
8. Article metadata, canonical and `TechArticle`/appropriate structured data.

## Success criteria after 90 days
- All 20 priority articles published and internally connected.
- Search Console data available for the new content and existing priority pages.
- Growing impressions across target topic clusters.
- Measurable query discovery beyond the current tracked terms.
- Improved organic entry paths into G-NeMa product pages.
- No regression in the existing technical SEO audit: 0 errors / 0 warnings.
